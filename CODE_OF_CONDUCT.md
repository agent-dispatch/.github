# Code of Conduct

AgentDispatch follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- respecting different viewpoints and experience levels
- giving and receiving constructive technical feedback
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and design tradeoffs rather than people
- taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or conduct
- publishing private information without permission
- sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, discussions, or community spaces
- any behavior that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcoming

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer if available.

Reports should include:

- what happened
- where it happened
- when it happened
- any relevant links or screenshots
- whether immediate action is needed

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and avoid exposing reporters unnecessarily.
